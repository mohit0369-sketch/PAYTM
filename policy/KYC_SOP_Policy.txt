KYC MERCHANT ONBOARDING SOP

Purpose:
This policy defines the minimum requirements for merchant KYC onboarding and how incomplete, risky, and ambiguous cases should be handled.

Required Documents:
1. Identity Proof
2. Address Proof
3. Business Proof

Normal Incomplete Case:
- One or more required documents are missing.
- No risk flag is present.
- The case can receive an automated follow-up requesting the missing documents.

High-Risk Case:
A case is considered high-risk when:
- A risk flag is explicitly present.
- There is a repeated failed resubmission.
- There is a name or identity mismatch.
- There is suspicious or conflicting information.

Handling Rules:
1. If required documents are missing and there is no risk flag:
   - Send a follow-up to the merchant.
   - Ask specifically for the missing documents.
   - Do not escalate unless a risk issue appears.

2. If a risk flag is present:
   - Do not automatically approve or close the case.
   - Do not make a final decision about the merchant.
   - Escalate the case to a human reviewer.
   - Include the risk flag and missing documents in the escalation.

3. If the information is ambiguous or conflicting:
   - Do not guess.
   - Escalate to a human reviewer.

Verification:
After a merchant submits missing documents:
- Check whether the previously missing documents are now present.
- If all required documents are present and no risk issue exists:
  mark the case as ready for closure.
- If documents are still missing:
  continue the follow-up process.
- If a risk issue appears:
  escalate to a human reviewer.

Important:
The AI must use this policy as its source of truth.
The AI must not invent KYC requirements.
Risky or ambiguous cases must be escalated rather than automatically resolved.
